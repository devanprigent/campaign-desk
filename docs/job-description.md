## Merkl

Finance is moving onchain: stablecoins, tokenized stocks and funds, onchain payments. Each of these comes with money that has to reach someone, whether interest to stablecoin holders, dividends to shareholders or rewards to users. Merkl is the infrastructure that computes who is owed what, and pays them.

Stablecoin issuers, fintechs, neobanks, tokenized funds and DeFi protocols use Merkl to calculate and send yield, dividends, rewards and payouts. The product runs through two channels: the Merkl app, used by 100,000 to 150,000 people every month, and an API integrated in white label by players like Coinbase and Robinhood, which handles around 500 requests per second and reaches millions of end users.

Merkl is a team of around 20 people in Paris, founded by three X and Stanford alumni and operating since 2021. The company is profitable and fully independent: it bought back almost all of its early investors and sets its own roadmap. Despite the onchain execution, it is first and foremost a web2 engineering company building financial infrastructure.

## The role

- You own product features end to end: the interface, the API routes, the database, and the architecture choices between services.
- You work closely with the product lead (PM, around 15 years of experience), who explores and scopes the problems. You challenge the proposed direction when you see a better way, then own the execution.
- You help structure the team's work on large projects and support the junior engineers.
- Seniority here is about posture rather than years: autonomy, initiative, judgement and composure.
- The organisation is flat, with one manager per team. Evolution towards a product tech lead role and moving between teams are both possible.

## What you'll do

- Build and evolve the Merkl app and the client interface where companies create and manage their distributions.
- Design and ship the API routes and data access behind them, with attention to latency and query performance.
- Turn Figma mockups or prototypes into polished, responsive interfaces, and build features from scratch when there is no detailed spec.
- Take part in design reviews on large features and bring a technical point of view early in scoping.
- Debug across front end and back end, and own the quality of what you ship (self-QA is part of the job).
- Lead the response during incidents and help the team stay calm.

## The team

The Product team gathers the product lead, a few engineers and a junior engineer who just joined. Large projects go through design reviews that involve people from other teams. The wider company includes Core Engine, Special Projects, Security (which supports infra and DevOps) and Sales. The culture rests on three values: sobriety, reliability and agility. People work with context rather than control, nobody micromanages, and egos stay small. The team is AI-native, with shared skills and review agents improved every week.

## The stack

- Languages: TypeScript across front and back, Go on some critical services
- Data: Postgres
- Infra: Kubernetes, Google Cloud
- Architecture: one monorepo and many microservices
